# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We are committed to maintaining a respectful, professional, and inclusive environment for everyone.

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Welcome different perspectives.
- Give useful technical feedback.
- Focus on ideas rather than individuals.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality.
- Avoid harassment and discriminatory language.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment or intimidation.
- Personal attacks.
- Discrimination.
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Publishing private information.
- Sexualized or threatening content.
- Unprofessional conduct in issues or pull requests.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported privately through the maintainer's GitHub profile:

https://github.com/Dev-moe-kyawaung

Reports will be reviewed fairly and handled as confidentially as practical.
